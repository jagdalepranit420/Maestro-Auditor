    🎛️ BandLab, OBRA & GarageBand — Step-by-Step EQ & Noise Guide

    📱 BandLab Step-by-Step EQ & Mastering

    1. Open BandLab → tap + (Create) → choose Import File and load your lossless WAV file.
    2. Tap FX (Effects) at the bottom → tap + → select EQ → Graphic EQ.
    3. Adjust ONLY the bands flagged Yellow/Red in Brain 1 X-Ray using gentle moves (±1.5 to ±2 dB):
    Band (Hz)When to AdjustExact Action
    60–90 Hz (Bass/Kick)Bass feels heavy or masks vocalsPull down -1.5 dB
    250–300 Hz (Mud)Sound feels boxy, muddy, or cloudedCut -1.5 to -2.5 dB
    1–1.8 kHz (Nasal/Machine)Vocals sound nasal, honky, or roboticGentle cut -1.5 dB
    3–4 kHz (Ear Fatigue)Harsh on repeated listeningGentle cut -1.5 to -2 dB
    6–7.5 kHz (AI Shimmer/Sib)Piercing 'S/Sh' or metallic AI shimmerCut -1.5 to -2 dB
    10–12 kHz (Air/Silk)Track sounds muffled or dullBoost slightly +1.5 dB
    🔥 BandLab Mastering Rule: Use Clarity, Universal, or Tape. AVOID Fire — it boosts bass and buries vocals.

    🔇 Removing Noise, Hiss & AI Machine Sound in Vocals

    1. Light Hiss / Hum (BandLab): Track menu (⋮) → Denoise, or gentle FX → Dynamics → Noise Gate (do not cut natural vocal breaths).
    2. Metallic AI Vocal Shimmer: Cut -1.5 to -2 dB at 6–7.5 kHz and -1.5 dB at 1–1.8 kHz (nasal), plus light De-Esser.
    3. Deep Robotic / Machine Vocals: EQ alone cannot fix synthetic vocoder vocals — paste the Master Report into Gemini (Neon/Pro) to run a clean Suno Cover (Audio 75–80% • Style 45–55% • Weirdness 15–25% • Variety 0%) with strict anti-robot Exclude shields.
